Tools and Utilities

Once you have selected the Tools and Utilities area you are shown two listings . The list of grey buttons on the left are the areas the provided programs have been sorted into. The white area on the right is a list of programs associated with the selected area. If you click on any of the names on the right hand side of the screen the appropriate program, program installation, or folder will be opened.9a) on or off.
